CHARLS C17 endpoint resolution - independent sensitivity branch, 2026-10-08

1. Scope and outcome
This module builds auditable interval-censored mortality endpoints from actual
participant-level alive/dead evidence. It does not invent exact blood, visit,
or death dates and does not overwrite Table1, Meta, ED3b, ED8b, CURRENT, or Word.

The original 11,601-person common Table1 sample and approved score scales are
held fixed: SD(DISCO)=1.60976585242089; SD(log(DM))=0.39031674165343.
Adjustment: linear age, sex, smoking, drinking, and binary education. Education
is inherited from the accepted Table1 preparation, including its approved mode
imputation. No BMI, physical activity, or additional covariate was added here.

Observed-wave branch: N=11,370, deaths=1,096. The 231 baseline-only participants
are excluded because no post-baseline alive/dead evidence exists. Among the
1,088 previously unverified 2018 censored records, 617 are confirmed alive in
2015 and 240 in 2013, so they are censored at their actual last observed wave.
There are zero contradictions between the 2013/2015/2018 wave status sources.

Strict retrospective branch: N=11,370, deaths=1,106. Ten unambiguous Gregorian
deaths reported in the 2020 exit interview occurred in 2015-2017, after baseline
and before all 2018 interviews, with no later alive contradiction. They are
incorporated conservatively as (last alive wave, before2018 wave] intervals.
One previously baseline-only person is recovered; one contradictory record
(reported Gregorian death before a documented later alive interview) is
isolated. Another 2011 death report in a baseline-only participant is not used
because its order relative to the blood draw cannot be proved. Deaths reported
in 2018 or in a lunar calendar are not silently added to the original window.
These are reported dates, not administratively certified death records.

The semiparametric proportional-hazards likelihood uses observed inspection
order and arbitrary baseline survival increments. Coordinates 0/2/4/7 label
the 2011/2013/2015/2018 observation waves, not precise individual elapsed dates.
Recoding to 0/1/2/3 leaves every fitted coefficient identical. Weibull and
exponential interval-PH fits are additional parametric checks that DO depend
on the nominal spacings; they are not the preferred interval model.

Each branch was run in two separate R processes. Both scores used 200 actual
participant-level bootstrap samples for covariance, with doRNG per-iteration
streams and fixed seeds. Point estimates, every bootstrap coefficient, SE,
CI and P agree exactly between repeats; all bootstrap matrices are finite;
model warning count=0. Raw-score versus standardized coefficient errors are
<2e-8 in the observed-wave branch. This is technical reproducibility, not a
claim that reported dates are exact or that PH assumptions are guaranteed.

3. Prediction measures and propagation
Ordinary Harrell C is not reported for these interval-censored endpoints.
The historical crude C values remain historical wave-assigned comparisons.
The optional identifiable-pair concordance is labelled separately; it uses
only pairs with upper_i <= lower_j, excludes uncertain ordering, counts ties
as one half, and is NOT an ordinary Harrell C or a confidence interval.

Meta sensitivity reuses the locked DerSimonian-Laird pooling function. Other
four cohort inputs are unchanged byte-for-value, with no cohort model refits.

5. Re-run and dependency details
R=Rscript. Official CRAN binary packages icenReg2.0.16 and
MLEcens0.1-7.1 were installed ONLY in this module's rlib. Source installation
failed because the machine lacks the configured Fortran link runtime; official
macOS arm64 binaries solved this without changing the global R library. Local
X11/Cairo libraries are absent, so plotting uses grDevices::pdf with Helvetica.
Statistics and colors were not changed to solve a graphics-device dependency.
The failed Cairo attempt's automatic Rplots.pdf was inspected, identified as
this module's curve plot, and moved into logs/qa/failed_cairo_default_device.pdf
for recoverable diagnostics; it is not a selected or deliverable figure.

6. What can close, and what remains an author choice
Can close technically: old object/ID provenance, local status/last-alive chain,
the inappropriate assumption that every unmatched2018 participant survived8
years, auditable interval endpoints, true interval-PH/parametric/Meta sensitivity,
strict recovery of10 retrospective deaths, repeat and figure QA.

Cannot claim: complete exact dates for everyone, exact attained-age follow-up
from blood, exact IPCW time-dependent AUC, or full raw date certification of
the historical Table1/ED3b/ED8b results. This is not an unbounded request to wait
for perfect dates: a workable interval analysis has actually been completed.

Author choice: put this verified branch into SI and retain the old primary
models with an explicit wave-assigned limitation, OR adopt the interval-PH
association as CHARLS primary and synchronize Table1/Meta/Methods/Response,
replacing attained-age legacy curves with the wave-interval display. No new
science choice was silently applied here. The conflicting record is isolated
in the sensitivity, not adjudicated as laboratory/registry truth.
