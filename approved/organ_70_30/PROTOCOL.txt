Shared 70:30 internal split with train-only scoring and model construction
Fixed on 2026-10-06 before any scores or test results from this new split

Reason and scope
The user requested 70:30 or 60:40. The primary experiment is fixed at 70:30, not a search between ratios for the highest test C-index. The previous 18,648/4,294 common-intersection experiment and its 1,380-check QA/PH results are retained unchanged as a separate benchmark. Previously excluded members with discordant Oh/Goeminne split roles can enter this new experiment if they satisfy the prespecified participant and endpoint rules. Oh, Goeminne and DISCO use one shared new split, not intersections of separately drawn splits.
This reuses UKB participants after earlier analyses and viewing of the previous test results. It is neither a never-seen holdout nor external validation. No CURRENT, main/ED10 b/c, original data, prior protocol or manuscript is overwritten.

Single deterministic split
Sort eligible stable numeric UKB IDs ascending before randomization. Use R RNGkind(Mersenne-Twister, Inversion, Rejection), seed=20261006 exactly once. Initial strata are sex x floor(age/10)*10 x death status. Sparse handling is fixed before counts/results: within each sex x death-status group, if any nonempty ten-year age bin has fewer than 4 participants, double all age-bin widths in that group to 20,40,80,... until every nonempty bin has at least 4; if the group itself has <4 participants, use one all-age bin in that group. Sex and death status never merge. Record the actual effective bins and counts, including any sparse groups.
Target overall Ntrain=floor(.70*Neligible). Initial stratum train count=floor(.70*Nstratum), bounded to [1,Nstratum-1] when Nstratum>=2; singleton strata go to train. Reconcile to the exact overall target using largest fractional remainder first (ascending lexical stratum key breaks ties), adding only below the allowed maximum; if bound corrections require removal, remove by smallest fractional remainder and lexical key only above the allowed minimum. Draw each stratum's train members by sample() in ascending lexical stratum order. Retain sorted original stable-ID order within train and within test, then assemble train first/test second. Do not choose a different seed, pool strata differently after results, or fit/test several splits and retain the best one.

Calibration, evaluation and uncertainty
All age residual fits, age+sex residual fits, sample SDs, clock CV and Cox coefficients are estimated in train and applied unchanged to test. No test-batch calibration. All training Cox fits use Efron ties. Fair primary C comparison is age+sex baseline; age+sex+DISCO; age+sex+Oh; age+sex+Goeminne; age+sex+DISCO+Oh; age+sex+DISCO+Goeminne. Use identical held-out members for every organ/method. Training association HRs are per training age-residual sample SD and must not be presented as held-out association tests. Joint TRAIN PH diagnostics remain mandatory; failed PH cannot be labelled PASS or justify constant HR. Do not change the model specification based on the new results.
Requested d display remains Original=DISCO+age vs Oh alone vs Goeminne alone and Accel=each TRAIN age-residual score alone. This display has unequal age information; fairness claims use the common age+sex comparison, not its larger differences. Test residual Pearson correlations use TRAIN age+sex nuisance fits, not test-fitted partial correlations.
Harrell C and paired differences use simultaneous survival::concordance IJ covariance conditional on fitted train models. CIs exclude repeated split, retraining, feature-selection and imputation variability. Report exact formulas, conditional uncertainty, PH and convergence warnings. No new time-varying/segmented HR fit in this frozen comparison.
