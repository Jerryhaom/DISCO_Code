ED7 competing-death sensitivity analysis, authorized 2026-10-06

Purpose: retain ED7 1-KM artwork and add Aalen-Johansen disease cumulative
incidence curves with death before diagnosis as a competing event.
This is an estimation-method sensitivity analysis, not certification of the
unrecovered original UKB diagnosis-date generation chain (audit C03/C04).

Primary population: the exact combined clinical/protein score object used by
reproduce_ED7_KM_facet.R, UKB.ClinicalProt.DISCO.rds. Preserve DISCO and DM
quartile definitions in the full score population; plot Q1 and Q4 separately.
DM is monotone under log transformation, so its quartiles are unchanged.
Eight endpoints and labels follow the actual ED7 plotting code.

Link disease and mortality data by verified unique participant identifiers.
Verify saved score follow-up/status against UKB.Mortality.csv, and preserve
all original input files. Report all mismatches or missing links.
The saved ages and disease times lie on monthly ticks. Mortality CSV numeric
rounding differs from exact month fractions by at most 4e-8 month. After
verifying this grid, use integer-month working times for both estimators and
the independent check, and convert axes back to years. No source values change.
Use a common observation end: minimum of the saved disease observation/event
time and the saved mortality observation/death time. No extension beyond the
earlier observed follow-up is permitted. Negative disease times are excluded
using the historical prevalent-disease proxy. Zero intervals are excluded
because they cannot form a valid positive follow-up interval; their true
baseline/incident classification remains unresolved without source dates.

Monthly disease and mortality times cannot establish within-month ordering.
Primary sensitivity: exclude target-disease events that occur in the same
recorded month as death, and exclude disease events recorded later than death.
Both exclusions are analysis-only and explicitly tabulated, not input edits.
Additional bounds retain same-month events with target-first versus death-first
ordering, while the after-death conflicts remain excluded pending source dates.
This protocol is fixed before the CIF and KM curves/results are inspected.

Fit Aalen-Johansen state probabilities using survival::survfit with the first
event coded as censor/disease/death. Primary time scale is years from baseline;
a companion uses attained age with delayed entry to match the ED7 x-axis.
Re-estimate 1-KM on the identical first-event risk set, censoring competing
deaths, to isolate the estimator difference. Do not compare unlike populations.
Show confidence intervals for CIF from the survfit multistate implementation;
report method, N, target events, competing deaths and at-risk counts separately.
Do not imply age/sex adjustment: these are unadjusted quartile curves.

Independently verify the AJ and KM point estimates by direct event-time
product updates, check state sums, monotonicity, and CIF <= 1-KM on the same
risk set. Re-run in two fresh R sessions and compare aggregate numeric output.
No individual IDs, fitted data objects or membership vectors are public output.
Final graphics reuse ED7 colors/line encodings and scientific ggplot theme,
with no small numeric annotations added. Supplementary figure number will be
assigned in the final manuscript sequence, not guessed from a filename.
